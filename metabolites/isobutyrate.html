Relevance in milk and feeding systems · diet study
Isobutyrate concentrations were significantly influenced by feeding systems (p < 0.001), with milk from TMR diets having higher concentrations, followed by PMR, and with GRS diets having the lowest concentrations. In the partial least squares discriminant analysis (PLS-DA), isobutyrate exhibited a variable importance in projection (VIP) score higher than 2.0 and in the biomarker analysis comparing GRS vs. TMR, it had an area under the curve (AUC) of 0.90, indicating excellent discriminatory power.
