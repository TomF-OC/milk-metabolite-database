Biochemical description
3-hydroxybutyrate is produced in the liver from acetyl-CoA during periods of high energy demands such as early lactation.
Relevance in milk and feeding systems · diet study
3-hydroxybutyrate concentrations were significantly influenced by feeding systems (p < 0.001), with higher concentrations in milk from TMR diets compared to GRS and PMR diets.
Seasonal trend · seasonality study
3-hydroxybutyrate concentrations were significantly influenced by season (p = 0.003), with the highest concentrations observed in winter and lower concentrations detected during spring, summer, and autumn.
