About Butyrate

Chemical class
Short-chain volatile fatty acid (VFA)
Biochemical description
Butyrate is one of the major microbial fermentation end-products in the rumen through microbial fermentation of dietary carbohydrates and fibres.
Relevance in milk and feeding systems · diet study
Butyrate concentrations were significantly influenced by feeding system (p < 0.001), with higher concentrations in milk from PMR group compared to GRS and TMR.
Seasonal trend · seasonality study
Butyrate did not differ significantly between seasons (p = 0.105).
