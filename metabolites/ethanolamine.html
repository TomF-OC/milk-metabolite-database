About Ethanolamine

Chemical class
Amino alcohol
Biochemical description
Component of membrane phospholipids. Ethanolamine is derived from serine and contributes to the synthesis of phosphatidylethanolamine.
Relevance in milk and feeding systems · diet study
Ethanolamine concentrations were not significantly influenced by feeding systems (p = 0.09).
