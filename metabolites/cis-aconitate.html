Biochemical description
Cis-aconitate is an intermediate in the tricarboxylic acid (TCA) cycle, formed from citrate and converted to isocitrate.
Relevance in milk and feeding systems · diet study
Cis-aconitate concentrations were significantly influenced by feeding systems (p < 0.001), with milk from PMR diets having higher concentrations compared to TMR, and with GRS having the lowest concentrations.
