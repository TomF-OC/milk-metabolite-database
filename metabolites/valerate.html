Relevance in milk and feeding systems · diet study
Valerate concentrations were significantly influenced by feeding systems (p = 0.014), with concentrations in PMR diets higher than GRS and TMR.
Seasonal trend · seasonality study
Valerate did not differ significantly across seasons (p = 0.122).
