About Fucose

Chemical class
Monosaccharide
Biochemical description
Fucose is a component of glycoproteins and glycolipids. Fucosylated oligosaccharides contribute to the bioactivity of milk, especially the immune properties.
Relevance in milk and feeding systems · diet study
Fucose concentrations were significantly influenced by feeding systems (p = 0.027), with higher concentrations in PMR diets overall.
Seasonal trend · seasonality study
Fucose concentrations were significantly influenced by season (p < 0.001), with lower concentrations in summer compared with the other seasons.
