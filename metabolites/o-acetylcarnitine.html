About O-Acetylcarnitine

Chemical class
Esterified derivative of carnitine
Biochemical description
O-acetylcarnitine is formed by the acetylation of carnitine within the mitochondria and helps buffering excess of acetyl groups. In milk, O-acetylcarnitine is associated with antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties.
Relevance in milk and feeding systems · diet study
O-Acetylcarnitine concentrations were significantly influenced by feeding systems (p < 0.001), with higher concentrations in milk from GRS and PMR diets compared to TMR diets.
Seasonal trend · seasonality study
O-Acetylcarnitine concentrations were significantly influenced by season (p < 0.001), with concentrations decreasing from winter to summer and increasing slightly again in autumn.
