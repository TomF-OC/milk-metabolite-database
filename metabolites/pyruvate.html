About Pyruvate

Chemical class
α-keto acid
Biochemical description
Pyruvate is a key intermediate in the carbohydrate metabolism and is the end product of glycolysis. Pyruvate can be converted into lactate, acetyl-CoA, oxaloacetate, or alanine, and it serves as a substrate for gluconeogenesis to generate glucose.
Relevance in milk and feeding systems · diet study
Pyruvate concentrations were significantly influenced by feeding system (p < 0.001), with milk from PMR and TMR diets having higher concentrations compared to GRS.
Seasonal trend · seasonality study
Pyruvate did not differ significantly across seasons (p = 0.078).
