About Lactose

Chemical class
Disaccharide
Biochemical description
Lactose is the principal carbohydrate in milk. Lactose is synthesized in the mammary gland and is composed of glucose and galactose.
Relevance in milk and feeding systems · diet study
Lactose concentrations were not significantly influenced by feeding systems (p = 0.109).
Seasonal trend · seasonality study
Lactose concentrations were significantly influenced by season (p = 0.013), decreasing progressively from winter to autumn.
