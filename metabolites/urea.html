About Urea

Chemical class
Amide belongs to nitrogen-containing compounds
Biochemical description
Urea is the principal end product of protein and nitrogen metabolism in mammals. Urea is formed in the liver through urea cycle from ammonia and carbon dioxide and can be diffused into milk from plasma.
Relevance in milk and feeding systems · diet study
Urea concentrations were not significantly influenced by feeding systems (p = 0.085).
Seasonal trend · seasonality study
Urea concentrations were significantly influenced by season (p < 0.001), with lower concentrations in winter, followed by spring and summer, and higher in autumn.
