About Leucine

Chemical class
Branched-chain essential amino acid (BCAA)
Biochemical description
Leucine plays a key role in protein synthesis. Leucine provides energy and intermediates for the tricarboxylic acid (TCA) cycle.
Relevance in milk and feeding systems · diet study
Leucine concentrations were significantly influenced by feeding systems (p = 0.002), with higher concentrations in PMR and TMR diets compared to GRS diets.
