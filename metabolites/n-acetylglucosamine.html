About N-Acetylglucosamine

Chemical class
N-acetylated amino sugar
Biochemical description
N-Acetylglucosamine serves as a precursor and a structural component of N-glycans. In milk, these N-glycans contribute to immune protection.
Seasonal trend · seasonality study
N-Acetylglucosamine concentrations were significantly influenced by season (p < 0.001), decreasing progressively from winter to autumn.
