About Formate

Chemical class
Short-chain fatty acid
Biochemical description
Formate is produced during microbial fermentation in the rumen and serves as electron carrier. Methanogens and homoacetogens use formate as a source of electrons. 5
Relevance in milk and feeding systems · diet study
Formate concentrations were significantly influenced by feeding systems (p < 0.001), with higher concentrations in milk from GRS and PMR diets compared to TMR. In the partial least squares discriminant analysis (PLS-DA), formate exhibited a variable importance in projection (VIP) score higher than 1.5, with elevated concentrations associated with PMR diets.
Seasonal trend · seasonality study
Formate did not show significant seasonal variation (p = 0.071).
