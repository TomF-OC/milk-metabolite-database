About Citrate

Chemical class
Tricarboxylic acid
Biochemical description
Citrate is an intermediate of the tricarboxylic acid (TCA) cycle. Citrate plays an important role in fatty acid synthesis and calcium regulation in milk. Citrate concentrations in milk reflect the balance between synthesis and milk secretion.
Relevance in milk and feeding systems · diet study
Citrate concentrations were significantly influenced by feeding system (p < 0.001), with higher concentrations in the PMR diets in spring and summer and higher concentrations in GRS in autumn.
Seasonal trend · seasonality study
Citrate concentrations were significantly influenced by season (p < 0.001), with higher concentrations in winter and lower in spring, summer and autumn.
