About Acetone

Chemical class
Ketone
Biochemical description
Formed through decarboxylation of acetoacetate, process that occurs during negative energy balance when glucose availability is limited, leading to lipid mobilization.
Relevance in milk and feeding systems · diet study
Acetone concentrations varied significantly by diet (p < 0.001). The highest concentrations were found in the PMR group, whereas the GRS group had the lowest concentrations.
Seasonal trend · seasonality study
Acetone concentrations were significantly influenced by season (p < 0.001), with the highest concentrations in winter and the lowest in summer.
