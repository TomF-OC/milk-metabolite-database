About Betaine

Chemical class
Quaternary ammonium compound belong to organic nitrogen compounds
Biochemical description
Betaine is derived from choline metabolism and functions as a methyl group donor. It is also involved in osmoregulation, protecting cells against osmotic stress.
Relevance in milk and feeding systems · diet study
Betaine concentrations were significantly influenced by feeding system (p < 0.001), with higher concentrations in milk from PMR group, followed by TMR, and with GRS having the lowest concentrations.
Seasonal trend · seasonality study
Betaine concentrations were significantly influenced by season (p < 0.001), with higher concentrations in winter and autumn and lower in spring and summer.
