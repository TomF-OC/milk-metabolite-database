About Alanine

Chemical class
Non-essential amino acid belong to the group of nitrogenous compounds
Biochemical description
Alanine is synthesized in the muscle tissue by transamination of pyruvate and plays a key role in the amino sugar metabolism.
Relevance in milk and feeding systems · diet study
Alanine concentrations were significantly influenced by feeding system (p < 0.001), with PMR and TMR diets having higher concentrations than GRS diets. In the partial least squares discriminant analysis (PLS-DA), alanine exhibited a variable importance in projection (VIP) score higher than 1.0, with elevated concentrations associated with PMR diets.
Seasonal trend · seasonality study
Alanine concentrations were significantly influenced by season (p = 0.030), decreasing slightly from winter to autumn.
