About Tyrosine

Chemical class
Non-essential amino acid
Biochemical description
Tyrosine is involved in protein synthesis and is a precursor for catecholamines, melanin, and thyroid hormones. During catabolism, tyrosine is broken down into acetoacetate and fumarate, which feeds into the tricarboxylic acid (TCA) cycle and supports energy production.
