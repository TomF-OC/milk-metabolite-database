About Dimethylamine

Chemical class
Aliphatic amine belonging to nitrogenous compounds
Biochemical description
Dimethylamine arises from the microbial degradation of choline in the rumen.
Relevance in milk and feeding systems · diet study
Dimethylamine concentrations were significantly influenced by feeding system (p = 0.004), with higher concentrations in milk from PMR diets compared to GRS and TMR.
