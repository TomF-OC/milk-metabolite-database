About Creatinine

Biochemical description
Creatinine is formed from creatine in the muscle tissue and it is associated with negative energy balance (NEB).
Relevance in milk and feeding systems · diet study
Creatinine concentrations were significantly influenced by feeding system (p < 0.001), with higher concentrations in milk from PMR diets compared to GRS and TMR in spring and summer, and with similar concentrations in autumn.
Seasonal trend · seasonality study
Creatinine concentrations were influenced by season (p < 0.001), decreasing significantly from winter to summer and autumn.
