About Choline

Chemical class
Quaternary ammonium salt
Biochemical description
Choline is involved in the phospholipid synthesis, acetylcholine production, and methyl-group transfer. Choline plays an important role in lipid transport and cell membrane integrity.
Relevance in milk and feeding systems · diet study
Choline concentrations were significantly influenced by feeding system (p < 0.001), with higher concentrations in GRS diets, followed by TMR and with the lowest concentrations in PMR diets.
Seasonal trend · seasonality study
Choline concentrations were significantly influenced by season (p < 0.001), with lower concentrations in winter and spring compared to summer and autumn.
