About sn-Glycero-3-phosphocholine

Chemical class
Glycerophospholipid
Biochemical description
Sn-glycero-3-phosphocholine (GPC) arises from the breakdown of phosphatidylcholine and is a reservoir of choline for the phospholipid synthesis, being involved in the membrane synthesis.
Relevance in milk and feeding systems · diet study
GPC concentrations were significantly influenced by feeding system (p < 0.001). In summer and autumn, milk from TMR diets exhibited higher GPC concentrations than PMR diets, with the lowest concentrations observed in GRS diets. In spring, PMR and TMR diets had lower GPC concentrations compared to GRS diets.
Seasonal trend · seasonality study
sn-Glycero-3-phosphocholine concentrations were significantly influenced by season, with a slight decrease from winter to autumn (p = 0.048).
