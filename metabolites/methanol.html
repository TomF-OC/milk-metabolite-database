About Methanol

Chemical class
Alcohol
Biochemical description
Methanol is produced through microbial fermentation of pectins in the rumen. Methanol is typically metabolized to formaldehyde and then to formate.
Relevance in milk and feeding systems · diet study
Methanol concentrations were significantly influenced by feeding systems (p < 0.001), with milk from TMR diets having the highest concentrations, followed by PMR diets, and with GRS diets having the lowest concentrations. In the partial least squares discriminant analysis (PLS-DA), methanol exhibited a variable importance in projection (VIP) score higher than 1.5, with elevated concentrations associated with TMR diets.
