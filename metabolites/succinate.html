About Succinate

Chemical class
Dicarboxylic acid
Biochemical description
Intermediate in the tricarboxylic acid (TCA) cycle. Succinate is formed from succinyl-CoA and is oxidized to fumarate.
Relevance in milk and feeding systems · diet study
Succinate concentrations were significantly influenced by feeding systems (p < 0.001), with higher concentrations in milk from PMR and TMR diets compared to GRS diets. In the partial least squares discriminant analysis (PLS-DA), succinate exhibited a variable importance in projection (VIP) score higher than 1.5, with elevated concentrations associated with PMR diets.
Seasonal trend · seasonality study
Succinate did not vary significantly between seasons (p = 0.311).
